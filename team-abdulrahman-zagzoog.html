About

Abdulrahman Zagzoog is a dedicated Occupational Health, Safety, Environmental, and Quality (HSEQ) professional and technical trainer. With a strong commitment to industry standards and regulatory compliance, he specializes in delivering high-quality training and consulting services designed to foster safer, more efficient workplaces.

His expertise spans a wide range of international and local safety frameworks, including OSHA (CFR 1910) and IOSH standards, alongside ISO management systems (such as ISO 9001, 14001, and 45001) and the Saudi Building Code. Through his work at the Knowledge Seekers Training Center, he focuses on equipping professionals with the practical skills and certifications necessary to excel in industrial and technical environments.

With a proven track record of elevating operational standards across the industrial sector in Saudi Arabia, Abdulrahman specializes in the design, implementation, and management of comprehensive Quality (QMS), Health & Safety (OHSMS), and Environmental (EMS) management systems. His expertise spans over 300 facilities nationwide, ensuring regulatory compliance and fostering cultures of safety and excellence.
